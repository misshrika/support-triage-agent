# Shipping Policy

- Standard shipping takes 5-8 business days within the continental US.
- Expedited shipping (2-3 business days) is available at checkout for an
  additional fee.
- Orders over $75 qualify for free standard shipping automatically.
- We do not currently ship outside the United States and Canada.
- Once an order ships, the customer receives a tracking number by email
  within 24 hours.
- If a tracking number shows no movement for more than 5 business days,
  customers should contact support so we can open a carrier investigation.
- Address changes can only be made within 1 hour of placing an order,
  before the warehouse begins fulfillment. After that, the order cannot be
  redirected and the customer must wait for delivery or refuse the package.
- Signature is required for orders over $300.

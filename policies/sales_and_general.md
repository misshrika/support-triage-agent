# Sales Inquiries & General Policies

- Bulk/wholesale orders (20+ units of a single item) qualify for a 15%
  discount; route these inquiries to the wholesale team at
  wholesale@northbridgehome.example, not standard support.
- Price-match requests are honored against identical items from Target,
  Walmart, or Wayfair, within 14 days of purchase, with a screenshot or link
  as proof.
- We do not offer price adjustments for items that go on sale after
  purchase, except within the standard 14-day price-match window above.
- Gift cards never expire and can be combined with promotional discounts.
- Customers asking about restock dates for out-of-stock items should be
  directed to sign up for the "notify me" email alert on the product page —
  support does not have access to exact restock ETAs beyond what's shown
  there.
- Loyalty program members (Northbridge Plus) get free expedited shipping on
  all orders regardless of order size.

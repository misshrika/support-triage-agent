# Technical Support & Product Issues

## Smart Home Devices (Northbridge Connect line)

- If a device won't connect to Wi-Fi, first-line troubleshooting is: power
  cycle the device (hold reset button 10 seconds), confirm the home Wi-Fi is
  2.4GHz (our devices do not support 5GHz-only networks), and re-run setup
  through the Northbridge app.
- If the device connects but shows "offline" intermittently, this is almost
  always a router range/interference issue, not a device defect — advise
  moving the device closer to the router as a first step.
- Devices with a persistent solid red light after a factory reset are
  hardware-faulty and qualify for a warranty replacement, not just
  troubleshooting.

## Warranty

- All Northbridge Connect devices carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty
  against defects.
- Warranty claims require proof of purchase (order number is sufficient).
- Accidental damage (drops, water damage) is not covered under warranty.
- Approved warranty replacements ship within 2 business days at no cost to
  the customer; the defective unit does not need to be returned unless
  requested.

## Escalation

- Any technical issue that has already gone through two rounds of
  troubleshooting without resolution should be escalated to a Tier 2
  specialist, not repeated a third time by the initial support agent.
- Safety-related complaints (device overheating, smoke, sparking) must be
  escalated immediately and are never handled as a standard troubleshooting
  ticket.

# Refund Policy

Northbridge Home Goods accepts returns within 30 days of delivery for a full
refund, provided the item is unused and in its original packaging.

- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days
  of us receiving the returned item.
- Sale items marked "Final Sale" are not eligible for return or refund.
- Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error
  (wrong item shipped, item arrived damaged/defective).
- Customers must request a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number
  before shipping an item back. Items returned without an RMA may be
  refused or delayed.
- Refunds for orders paid by gift card are issued as store credit, not cash.
- Custom or made-to-order items are non-refundable unless defective.
- If a package is lost in transit, we file a claim with the carrier; the
  customer is refunded or reshipped once the claim is resolved (typically
  10-14 business days).
